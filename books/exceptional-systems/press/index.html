Descriptions,
three lengths.

25 words

Chris and Marcus Seegers on building a business that runs on documentation and discipline instead of heroic effort and tribal knowledge.

50 words

The businesses that consume the life they were supposed to fund all fail the same way: the owner is the system. Exceptional Systems is the fix — four phases run as a quarterly loop, ending in the one most operating systems leave out. Monetize It.

150 words

Every business owner who builds something real with their hands, their talent and their years deserves better than running out of time holding it. The companies that consume the very life they were supposed to fund all fail the same way: the owner is the system. Every decision routes through one person, every process lives in one head, and the company cannot be handed to anyone — not a buyer, not a successor, not a Tuesday off.

Exceptional Systems is the fix, written as a loop you run rather than a theory you agree with: Dream It, Build It, Optimize It, Monetize It, cycled quarterly and annually, each pass compounding on the last. It ends with the phase most operating systems skip — the deliberate harvest, whether that is a sale, a transition or a restructure.

Chris Seegers founded the Exceptional Companies family office. Marcus Seegers is a Co-Founder of Exceptional Business Advisors, where he owns operations and systems.

For hosts and interviewers

Five questions you can ask cold

01You say the owner is the system in most struggling companies. What is the first sign of that from the outside?
02People, Process, Profit. What actually breaks when someone reverses the order?
03Part four is Monetize It, and most operating systems stop before that. Why do they, and why did you not?
04You wrote this with your brother, who runs the operations side. What does he see that you do not?
05The book ends with homework, not inspiration. What happens in the first ninety days?

Use them, change them, ignore them. They are here so you do not have to read the book to run a good interview — though we hope you do.

Chris Seegers — long form, 124 words

Chris Seegers is a business owner with a long track record of starting and buying companies and building them into successful, self-governed entities. He founded the Exceptional Companies family office, is an active owner in many different businesses, and has served on leadership teams that have raised and deployed over $750 million in investor capital. Chris is a “capitalist missionary,” and his heart is activating and equipping others to live exceptional lives.

Chris is a follower of Jesus, husband to his best friend Tara, and father to Jed, Chisum, and Lillian. He is a sibling to seven, and loves deep, authentic friendships and old books. Chris and his family live in Colorado Springs surrounded by mountains and amazing people.

Chris Seegers — short form, 50 words

Chris Seegers founded the Exceptional Companies family office and has served on leadership teams that raised and deployed over $750 million in investor capital. A self-described “capitalist missionary,” he lives in Colorado Springs with his wife and co-author Tara and their three children.

Marcus Seegers — short form

Marcus Seegers is co-author of Exceptional Systems and co-leader of the Exceptional Companies ecosystem. He is a Co-Founder of Exceptional Business Advisors, where he owns operations and systems — running, in a real company with real people in it, the operating system this book documents.
