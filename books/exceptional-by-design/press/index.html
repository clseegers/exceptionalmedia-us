Descriptions,
three lengths.

25 words

Chris and Tara Seegers on designing a life on purpose — eight pillars, four phases, and an assessment that shows you where you actually stand.

50 words

Most people design their business and improvise their life. Exceptional by Design treats a whole life the way an operator treats a company: in pillars you can name, phases you can run, and disciplines you can measure. Seventeen chapters, written to be worked rather than read.

150 words

Most people design their business and improvise their life. In Exceptional by Design, Chris and Tara Seegers hand over the framework they built for themselves after a year they did not plan and would not repeat — and then used to move their family from Texas to Colorado, restructure their businesses, and build the life they had been describing to each other for a decade.

The book runs on eight pillars — Faith, Family, Fitness, Finances, Fulfillment, Fun, Freedom and Business — across four phases: Dream It, Build It, Optimize It, Live It. Seventeen chapters, each one an exercise rather than an argument. It is written to be worked, not read.

Chris Seegers founded the Exceptional Companies family office. Tara Seegers is a Certified Financial Planner™ named to Forbes’ Top Women Wealth Advisors Best-In-State list. They live in Colorado Springs with their three children.

For hosts and interviewers

Five questions you can ask cold

01The book opens with a year you did not plan. What did that year teach you that no business lesson had?
02You say most people design their business and improvise their life. Where does that usually show up first?
03Eight pillars is a lot to hold at once. What happens to someone who is exceptional in six and neglecting two?
04“Fence to the support post” came from building two miles of fence by hand at twenty-four. How does that translate for someone staring at a ten-year vision?
05You wrote this with your wife. What did the two of you disagree about?

Use them, change them, ignore them. They are here so you do not have to read the book to run a good interview — though we hope you do.

Chris Seegers — long form, 124 words

Chris Seegers is a business owner with a long track record of starting and buying companies and building them into successful, self-governed entities. He founded the Exceptional Companies family office, is an active owner in many different businesses, and has served on leadership teams that have raised and deployed over $750 million in investor capital. Chris is a “capitalist missionary,” and his heart is activating and equipping others to live exceptional lives.

Chris is a follower of Jesus, husband to his best friend Tara, and father to Jed, Chisum, and Lillian. He is a sibling to seven, and loves deep, authentic friendships and old books. Chris and his family live in Colorado Springs surrounded by mountains and amazing people.

Chris Seegers — short form, 50 words

Chris Seegers founded the Exceptional Companies family office and has served on leadership teams that raised and deployed over $750 million in investor capital. A self-described “capitalist missionary,” he lives in Colorado Springs with his wife and co-author Tara and their three children.

Tara Seegers — long form

Tara Seegers is a nationally recognized wealth advisor and Certified Financial Planner™ who serves multi-generational families and business owners across the United States. Named to Forbes’ “Top Women Wealth Advisors Best-In-State” list, Tara specializes in creating clarity from complexity and helping families build legacies that last for generations.

Tara is a follower of Jesus, wife to Chris, and mother to Jed, Chisum, and Lillian. She believes exceptional wealth planning starts with understanding what truly matters. Tara and her family live in Colorado Springs, where they’re designing an exceptional life together.
